Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Hive Inc. is free, with no ads and nothing to buy.

Does it need the internet?

No, it plays offline. When the phone is online and anonymous stats are on, the game sends a few small play statistics (see the privacy policy). You can switch them off in Settings.

Will I lose my progress?

Your apiary saves itself on your phone and goes along with Android's backup. To move it to another phone by hand, open Settings and use "Copy backup code", then "Restore from code" on the other phone.

Which phones can run it?

Phones and tablets with Android 7.0 or newer. On an older phone, set Graphics to Low in Settings for a smoother game.

I found a bug or have an idea

Please write to mapacz.dev@gmail.com, ideally with your phone's model and the game's version (shown at the bottom of Settings).

Need help?

Write to us and we'll get back to you. The privacy policy explains the little the game collects.
